# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 30, 2026

## Corsair Privacy Policy

Thank you for using **Corsair**.

Corsair is a developer tool designed to inspect and analyze HTTP/HTTPS network traffic for debugging, testing, and development purposes. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Corsair handles information while you use the extension.

---

# Information We Collect

Corsair is designed with privacy in mind.

**Corsair does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information with the developer or any third party.**

All network inspection is performed locally within your browser unless you explicitly export data.

---

# Website Content

To provide its core functionality, Corsair processes website content that is necessary for network inspection, including:

- HTTP requests
- HTTP responses
- Request headers
- Response headers
- Query parameters
- Cookies
- JSON payloads
- HTML responses
- Images and other response content

This information is processed **only** to display network activity requested by the user during debugging sessions.

Website content is **never transmitted to Corsair servers**.

---

# Personal Information

Corsair does **not** collect or process personal information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Postal address
- Government identification numbers
- Payment information
- Health information
- Authentication credentials
- Browsing history
- GPS or precise location information

---

# Local Data Storage

Corsair may store information locally on your device to improve your experience.

This may include:

- Extension settings
- User preferences
- Saved filters
- Captured sessions explicitly saved by the user

All stored data remains under the user's control.

---

# Exported Data

Corsair allows users to export captured network sessions.

Exported files are created **only when explicitly requested by the user** and are saved to a location selected by the user.

Corsair does not upload exported files.

---

# Permissions

Corsair requests only the permissions required to provide its functionality.

These may include:

- **activeTab** – Inspect the currently active tab after user interaction.
- **tabs** – Associate captured requests with browser tabs.
- **sidePanel** – Display the extension interface.
- **host permissions** – Inspect HTTP/HTTPS traffic for websites selected by the user.
- **storage** – Save preferences and exported sessions.
- **debugger** – Capture network requests and responses for debugging purposes.

These permissions are never used for advertising, tracking, or profiling users.

---

- Share user data with analytics providers
- Build advertising profiles
- Track users across websites
- Use captured data for marketing purposes
